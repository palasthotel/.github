# ✨ Palasthotel – Gesellschaft für digitale Pracht mbH ✨
_(roughly: Company for Digital Splendour)_

**Palasthotel builds digital products.** We consult, plan, design and build websites, content management systems, software and apps.

## 💡 How we work

We love simple structures and clarity. Our approach is built for the long run: good consulting, lean&clean code and a high degree of reusability.

We choose the specific technologies for each project together with our clients, always tailored to their requirements. Out of deep conviction, we rely on **open source** rather than proprietary software.

## 👥 Who we are

Founded in Bielefeld in 2003, we now operate as a limited company (GmbH) with offices in **Hamburg** and **Bielefeld**. Our team consists of 15 permanently employed developers (3 women, 12 men) and 5 freelancers, spread across Berlin, Bielefeld, Hamburg, Münster, Würzburg and Mannheim.

We also collaborate with our friends at the tech agencies [about:source](https://aboutsource.net) and [erdfisch](https://erdfisch.de), as well as with [freistil.it](https://freistil.it), specialists in hosting high-traffic systems.

We think and work across disciplines: almost everyone on our team combines a background in computer science with a degree in design, journalism or the humanities. This is exactly what our clients value: technology, concept, editorial strategy and design, all from a single source.

## 🤝 Our clients

Most of our clients are:

- **Publishers**, such as Reise Know-How Verlag
- **Foundations and NGOs**, such as Heinrich-Böll-Stiftung, Campact and Amadeu Antonio Stiftung
- **Media companies and projects**, such as Urlaubsarchitektur.de, Netzpolitik.org and Dekoder.org
